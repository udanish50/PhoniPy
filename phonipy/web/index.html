    TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

    Ten measurements.
    One clear methodology.

    PhoniPy maps detected fundamental frequencies to individual musical notes and measures the duration and harmonic purity of stable intervals.

    Read the technical specification ↗
    01 / DETECT

    Listen for the fundamental.

    Short overlapping windows identify a periodic pitch candidate and measure its harmonic-series energy.

    02 / CONFIRM

    Find the stable intervals.

    Eight consecutive same-note frames qualify by default, corresponding to a nominal 138 ms hold.

    03 / MEASURE

    Describe the recording.

    Ten defined measures describe timing, stability, fragmentation, harmonic energy, and reference-note offset.
